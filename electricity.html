Electricity

You need electricity for the IE Metal Press that makes bullet casings, and for most IE and Refined Storage machines. The cheapest source in this pack has no moving parts and no fuel: a thermoelectric generator between something hot and something cold.

The one formula to know

A thermoelectric generator gives √(temperature difference) FE/t for every pair of opposite faces where one side is hot and the other cold. That's with the pack's ×2 setting. Lava is 1300 K and water 300 K, so one pair gives 31 FE/t, and a generator can have up to 3 pairs.

Three stages

Early game

Thermoelectric row

62FE/t per generator
  • Recipe: 3 steel ingots, 5 constantan plates and 1 LV wire coil. No fuel. The lava and water are never used up.
  • Row: water, generator, lava, generator, water… Each generator gets 31 FE/t from its side neighbours. Add hot above and cold below, switching every other generator, and it gets 62.
  • Solid blocks work too: a magma block counts as 1300 K, like lava. Blue ice is 200 K, packed ice 240, ice 260 and snow 273.
  • Lava: buckets from lava lakes, or a superheated mixer turns cobblestone into lava later.

8 generators give about 500 FE/t, enough for the Metal Press and an early IE setup.

Mid game

Alternators on the boiler

1,440FE/t from 8
  • Alternator (Crafts & Additions): 180 FE/t at 256 RPM, and it takes 8,192 SU to run. That's 32 SU per RPM, with output going up and down with speed.
  • On a level 4 coal boiler: 65,536 SU ÷ 8,192 = 8 alternators, or 1,440 FE/t. The boiler gives 64 RPM, so gear up twice to reach 256.
  • Compact: alternators only take rotation on one face, so line up gearboxes and put an alternator on both sides of each.
  • In this pack the alternator is a 5×5 mechanical-crafter recipe: 2 andesite alloy, 6 iron plates, 3 copper coils, an iron rod and a capacitor.

Or keep it fuel-free: 20 thermoelectrics (62 each) give 1,240 FE/t.

Late game

Big banks and reactors

6,480FE/t from 36 alternators
  • Max boiler (294,912 SU) drives 36 alternators: 6,480 FE/t.
  • Uranium thermoelectrics: a uranium block counts as 2000 K. Against water that's 41 FE/t per pair, and the blocks are never used up.
  • Extreme Reactor: runs on uranium ingots. In this pack its parts need Refined Storage controllers and advanced processors. Its output depends on its design, and the controller screen shows it.
  • Skip the IE Diesel Generator for steady power: it gives 8,192 FE/t but burns 80 mB of biodiesel every second.

Use alternators if you already run a big boiler, and the reactor once Refined Storage is up.

The builds

8 thermoelectric generators, 62 FE/t each

  1. Lay a row: water, generator, lava, generator, water, generator, lava… Each generator sits between a hot and a cold block, which gives 31 FE/t.
  2. Above and below each generator, put a hot block on one side and a cold one on the other, switching every other generator. That adds a second pair, for 62 FE/t.
  3. Put stone between the fluid blocks on the top and bottom layers, and glass on the open sides, so water and lava never touch. With magma blocks and ice you don't need any of that.
  4. For 93 FE/t, add a third hot/cold pair on the north and south faces as well.
  5. Collect the power with IE wire connectors, or put a capacitor against the row.

8 alternators on a coal boiler

  1. Take the boiler's 64 RPM shaft through two large→small cogwheel steps to reach 256 RPM.
  2. Run that shaft into a row of 4 gearboxes, and put an alternator on the north and south face of each one, facing the gearbox.
  3. Each alternator makes 180 FE/t at 256 RPM: 1,440 FE/t from 8, using 65,536 SU, the whole level 4 boiler.
  4. Put a Crafts & Additions accumulator next to them, or wire them with C&A connectors. Each alternator pushes out up to 256 FE/t.

Storing it

Crafts & Additions accumulator

2,048,000 FE per block, and blocks join into tanks up to 3 wide and 5 tall (45 blocks, about 92 million FE). It moves 512 FE/t in and out.

IE capacitors

LV holds 1,000,000 FE, MV 4,000,000 and HV 8,000,000 with the pack's settings. They're good next to the Metal Press so it keeps running at night when the windmill slows down.
