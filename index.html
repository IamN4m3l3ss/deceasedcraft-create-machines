Create Machines

Small, cheap factory builds for the pack's own recipes: automated ammo and gun parts, plus the rotational power and electricity to run them. Every build has a 3D model you can turn around and cut into layers.

New world? Start here

Put up a 64-sail windmill for power. Then build the Metalworks line for plates and springs, and the ammo line. A row of thermoelectric generators between lava and water gives you electricity for the IE Metal Press without any fuel.

Pack rules that change things

Deployers cost an IE Electronic Component

The pack swaps Create's Electron Tube for IE's Electronic Component in every recipe, so deployers, brass funnels and mechanical crafters cost more than usual.

An empty deployer stops the belt

Items wait forever under a deployer with nothing in its hand. Keep every station stocked, or put a lever on it to switch it off.

Small water wheels make half

The pack's config cuts the small wheel to 128 SU. The large wheel still makes 512 SU.

You don't need a lake

A pump pulling from a 2×2 water pool never empties it: vanilla refills the source block straight away, and Create counts that as endless water.

Burners take fuel by hand only

Blaze burners don't accept fuel from funnels, hoppers or chutes. Feed them yourself, or use a deployer or a Mechanical Arm.
